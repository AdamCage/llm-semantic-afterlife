## s0_continuation_mechanisms

For each candidate generator, all three continuation mechanisms are attempted: raw text completion, assistant prefill, and instruction-framed chat. Records whether the model continues the text, its finish reason, the local-vs-API prompt token delta (which reveals a server-side chat template), and whether the output looks like meta-commentary rather than continuation.

- formats: [`html`](audit/s0_continuation_mechanisms.html), [`csv`](audit/s0_continuation_mechanisms.csv)
- source data: [`parquet`](audit/s0_continuation_mechanisms.data.parquet)
- runs: `s0-audit-continuation-20260830T072553Z-9787b3d3`
- git: `61ccafc823b2`

## s0_determinism

Exact-match and similarity rates over 5 identical seeded requests per model at temperature 0.7. This number, and not an assumption, determines the reproducibility level the paper may claim.

## s0_embeddings

## s0_reasoning_suppression

For each generator, several candidate ways of switching reasoning off are attempted and the resulting reasoning-token count, visible output length and max_tokens compliance recorded. Reasoning tokens are disqualifying here: the block appended to the window would be only the visible part of what the model generated, max_tokens would stop bounding the block, and the reasoning text is meta-commentary rather than free continuation.

- formats: [`html`](audit/s0_reasoning_suppression.html), [`csv`](audit/s0_reasoning_suppression.csv)
- source data: [`parquet`](audit/s0_reasoning_suppression.data.parquet)
- runs: `s0-audit-reasoning-20260830T072812Z-9787b3d3`
- git: `61ccafc823b2`

## s0_tokenizers

Round-trip integrity (decode(encode(x)) == x) for every generator tokenizer across ASCII, Unicode, whitespace and long-repetition probes, plus exactness of the tail operation the sliding window relies on. A failure invalidates W for that model.

- formats: [`html`](audit/s0_tokenizers.html), [`csv`](audit/s0_tokenizers.csv)
- source data: [`parquet`](audit/s0_tokenizers.data.parquet)
- runs: `s0-audit-tokenizers-20260830T071933Z-9787b3d3`

## msd_loglog

Mean squared displacement against lag, log–log, on L2-normalised chunk embeddings, post-horizon segment only. Thin grey lines are individual trajectories; the blue curve is the ensemble mean with a 95% bootstrap CI resampled over trajectories (n=1). The dashed line has slope 1, the free-diffusion reference.
