## degeneracy_verdicts

Per-trajectory degeneracy diagnostics. A chunk counts as looping when its 3-gram repetition rate exceeds 0.083, the 99th percentile of natural English prose chunked by the same tokenizer at the same size. A trajectory counts as degenerate when at least 50% of its post-horizon chunks are looping.

**Does not establish:** Degeneracy is a surface-form measure. A trajectory can be lexically varied and still semantically static, which is what the frozen-embedding check and the geometry pass are for.

## chapman_kolmogorov

Chapman–Kolmogorov max |T(kτ) − T(τ)^k| at the primary lag. Bars are labelled micro (k-means, pre-registered F6 bar 0.15) or macro (spectral coarse-graining). A micro-MSM above the dashed line fails F6. That is a sparse count-matrix test, not a claim that the process is non-Markov.

**Does not establish:** F6 is scored on the K-state micro-MSM only. Unused microstates get a self-loop; a single empty-versus-occupied discrepancy drives the max toward 1. The 0.15 bar is pre-registered, not calibrated to this K / n_frames regime, and is not scale-aware. Methodology's VAMP-reduced CK was not run. Macro CK is a different object: several F4 cells pass 0.15 after coarse-graining because a 2–4 state partition of a loop is easier to fit, not because H1 holds.

## implied_timescales

## leiden_msm_agreement

Adjusted Rand index between Leiden communities (time-blind mutual-kNN on PCA of raw embeddings) and MSM macrostate assignments, with a 95% CI from resampling trajectories. High agreement can mean both methods found the same states or that both collapsed to one region — read next to n_macro.

**Does not establish:** The CI resamples trajectories on frozen labels; it is not a refit CI. Leiden is not run in VAMP coordinates. UMAP is not used for any number. Cross-space ARI is 0 on qwen-raw because one space chose n_macro=1; that is arithmetic, not disagreement between two rich partitions.

## probability_currents

## protocol_by_quarter

Block fill and stop-token rate by quarter of each trajectory, then averaged across trajectories of the same generator with a 95% bootstrap CI. Quarters are even bins over steps, not tokens: fill and stop are per-request properties, and late steps shorten when the model starts hitting stop.

**Does not establish:** A run-level mean is not shown and must not be read off the figure. Drift inside a quarter is hidden. This is a protocol diagnostic, not a semantic measurement: a falling fill does not by itself mean the trajectory has reached a semantic fixed point.

## s30_surface_labels

Hand labels of S3.0 step-1, last-quarter, and last-step text for local-gemma-3-1b-pt at W=256, 12 turnovers (run s3-local-base-20260901T184812Z-cc80633b). Reviewer register = self-review of the prompt ('you've provided', 'well-structured', 'comprehensive overview'). Silence = whitespace / recursion marks as in gemma-4-31b. Verbatim loop = a ≥40-character span repeats in the same completion. Token lock = a short repeated token or phrase that is not whitespace/recursion ('I I', 'Is This', '/1/1'). Continuation = none of those. Sliding seed-echo = last block is a rotation of the first. last_step uses the same criterion as last_quarter. 0/8 open in the reviewer register; 0/8 last-quarter silence; 8/8 degenerate by the last quarter.

**Does not establish:** Labels are a stated criterion applied to quoted text, not a classifier. The degeneracy threshold was calibrated at 1024-token chunks; S3.0 chunks are 256 tokens. 8/8 have n-gram repetition 0.84–0.98, so the calibration mismatch cannot flip the looping verdict. W=256 does not transfer to W=4096. T=0.3 physics s1 and s2 are bit-identical; effective independent n at that cell is 1, not 2.

- source data: [`parquet`](surface/s30_surface_labels.data.parquet)
- runs: `s3-local-base-20260901T184812Z-cc80633b`
