# ADR-0031: Assemble the closed research into one publication manuscript

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-08
Type: editorial; not a research stage
Amends: [ADR-0030](ADR-0030-s13-paper-b-section.md), manuscript presentation only

## Context

The research ladder ends at S13. The user authorised preparation of a
unified publication manuscript after the final scientific assessment.
The checkout already contained an English assembly, while the Russian
manuscript and released PDFs still followed the earlier presentation.
Keeping Paper B as an appendix-like section after the Paper A discussion
would hide the final study's family-wise findings.

## Decision

1. Organise the English manuscript and its complete Russian mirror around
   textual repetition, initial-text dependence, completion heterogeneity,
   and the separate censored continuation cohort.
2. Use the independent occupancy replication as the primary occupancy
   evidence. Keep the original panel labelled archival and state that
   its source vectors cannot be recovered. Do not replace its numbers.
3. Preserve all closed stage reports, configurations, raw runs, classifiers,
   thresholds, and publication-grade experimental artifacts.
4. Update primary-source literature positioning. Do not claim recovered
   semantic memory, validated semantic macrostates, universal post-training
   effects, an attention mechanism, or permanent locks.
5. Build identified English, complete Russian, and anonymous English PDFs
   with the official TMLR style. Preserve the upstream style files and
   licence without modifications and record their upstream revision.
   The S12 published SVG receives a deterministic legend-group translation
   in an ignored build copy because the original legend covers marker edges.
   All remaining SVG nodes and the experimental artifact bundle stay unchanged;
   this is typesetting, not a new plot estimate or research run.
6. Run the existing S13 gate, verify sources and language parity, inspect
   the rendered pages, and obtain an independent manuscript reading.

There is no S14, no new generation or embedding, and no re-estimation of
the published measurements. The publication reading is not a claim of
external peer review or venue acceptance.

## Consequences

ADR-0030's append-only manuscript restriction is superseded for this
authorised editorial assembly. Its scientific constraints and the closed
research record remain in force. The final manuscript distinguishes the
two designs rather than pooling them.

## Reversal cost

The prior presentations remain in Git history. Restoring manuscript files
does not require changing or re-running any experiment.
