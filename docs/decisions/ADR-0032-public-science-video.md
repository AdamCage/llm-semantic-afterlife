# ADR-0032: Produce a public science video from the closed record

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-09
Type: editorial companion; not a research stage

## Context

The user authorised a Russian public science video covering motivation, methods,
completed work, findings and practical implications, using AI illustrations,
real plots, RouterAI narration, light background music and sound design, with
the finished video published on GitHub.

## Decision

- Keep the complete production source in `video/`, separately from the small
  publication-grade evidence in `artifacts/`. The Russian script is intentional
  public-facing content; build instructions and provenance stay English.
- Use closed sources, primary occupancy replication and family-wise synthesis.
  No new research generation, embedding, estimation or research stage is opened.
- Distinguish empirical plots, explanatory diagrams and AI artistic imagery.
- Narration uses the existing project ledger, settings and budget enforcement.
  The configured production cap is $2, subordinate to existing environment caps;
  it does not increase either cap. Print an estimate before calls. Persist raw
  requests, sanitized response metadata, binary responses and hashes.
- Built-in image generation belongs to the app's billing context, outside the
  RouterAI account. Do not invent a dollar price or record an unknown charge as
  zero. Preserve its tool provenance and mark project-dollar accounting N/A.
- Compose an original deterministic instrumental bed and subtle sound effects;
  lower music under narration. Do not copy or sample reference channels.
- Keep original research files unchanged. Release MP4 and downloadable audio
  separately from Git source; retain sources and finite-horizon qualifications.

## Consequences

The video is a communication artifact, not additional scientific evidence or a
new peer-review claim. Practical suggestions must be labelled as implications,
not tested interventions. Render and inspect scene layouts and audio balance
before publishing the completed release.
