## Scientific extensions

- **Logprob-level dynamics.** Providers can return `logprobs`/`top_logprobs`.
  Per-token entropy and surprise along a trajectory would give a second,
  representation-free view of the dynamics — order parameters that owe nothing
  to an embedding model. Cheap to add if the endpoints support it.
- **Cross-model trajectory transplant.** Take a trajectory generated by model A
  past its horizon, hand the tail to model B, and see whether B falls into A's
  state or its own. Directly probes whether states belong to the model or to the
  text.
- **Attractor stability under perturbation.** Inject a foreign block mid-flight
  and measure return time to the pre-perturbation macrostate — a semantic
  analogue of a relaxation experiment.
- **Language and register as coordinates.** If macrostates turn out to be
  distinguished by surface features (language, register, formatting) rather than
  content, that is a real and reportable negative result. Needs a dedicated
  feature battery.
- **Seed-content taxonomy.** Do factual, narrative, procedural and adversarial
  seeds have systematically different half-lives? Requires a larger, structured
  seed bank.
- **Theory link.** Connect the measured `T_½(W)` scaling to a simple
  self-replication model: information survives if the model re-emits it faster
  than the window evicts it, giving a predicted critical re-emission rate.

## Methodological

- **True sliding attention at scale.** Protocol P2 needs a GPU. Worth doing on
  rented hardware if the re-prompt caveat becomes the main reviewer objection.
- **Stride decoupled from block.** `S < B` (overlapping generation) or `S > B`
  would let us separate the effect of window advance from that of call
  granularity.
- **Chunk-boundary phase offset.** Deliberately offset chunk boundaries from
  step boundaries to prove that observed periodicities are not harness
  artifacts.
- **Prompt-cache-aware protocol.** A grow-then-truncate sawtooth window could
  cut input cost substantially where prefix caching is available, at the price
  of a window that oscillates rather than slides. Needs a formal statement and a
  demonstration of equivalence before it could carry results.

## Infrastructure

- **Streaming embeddings.** Embed chunks as they are generated instead of in a
  separate pass, to shorten the loop during long runs.
- **Live dashboard.** A small local viewer over `events.jsonl` for watching a
  multi-hour run without tailing logs.
- **Artifact diff.** `afterlife diff` between two stage artifact sets, for
  reviewing the effect of a methodological change.
- **DOI archiving.** Zenodo deposition of the artifact release at submission.

## Rejected (kept so we do not re-propose them)

- **HDBSCAN as the MSM discretisation.** Its `noise` label has no defensible
  transition semantics. Fine as an independent geometric method; not as the
  microstate assignment.
- **Overlapping chunks for a smoother trajectory.** Manufactures
  autocorrelation and therefore fake metastability.
- **Choosing the number of states by looking at a UMAP plot.** Not evidence.
- **Native context as the window variable.** Confounds architecture with memory;
  the whole point is to impose `W` and hold `θ` fixed.
