contact sheet. The title, content and footer stay within the 1080p safe area.

ASR timing as a guide. Narration ends before each scene's trailing pause.

Local multilingual Whisper recognised words through the end of all 19 scenes.
Exact normalised word anchors cover 82.9–96.4% per scene; unmatched words are
timed by interpolation. This is a timing aid, not a claim that every spoken
phoneme or subtitle timestamp has been manually checked.

## Final media checks

The finalizer performs a complete video/audio decode, checks total frame count
and duration, chapter count and subtitle stream, verifies source and voice
hashes, reconciles all 19 TTS receipts against the ledger, and measures mastered
loudness/true peak. Authoritative outcomes are in `production/manifest.json`,
`production/ffprobe.json` and `production/loudness-after.json`.

audited provider invoice. Built-in app image billing is outside that ledger.
